A few useful details.

What is a monthly event?

Page views and tracked behavior count toward your monthly allowance, shared across all your websites. Activity heartbeats are excluded. Duplicate events count once. The allowance resets each calendar month in UTC; new events beyond the limit are not accepted. All plans include the same analytics features.

How does yearly billing work?

Starter is $90 per year and Growth is $290 per year, paid annually in USD. The yearly toggle shows the full annual charge, not a monthly installment.

How do I get started?

Open your account, sign in with Google when it is enabled, and add your website. Account setup shows whether sign-in and paid billing are available for this deployment. Paid plans activate after payment confirmation.

Do I need a paid plan to see Revenue Stories?

No. Free includes Revenue Stories, Journeys, the live map, Website Footprints and the same analytics features. Choose a paid plan when you need more websites or a larger event allowance.
